EXAMINATION GUIDELINES

Welcome to the Institute of Board (IFB) Examination Portal. Please read these guidelines carefully. Failure to adhere to these protocols will result in your examination being flagged, voided, or referred for human moderation.

1. The 2027 Examination Periods

The IFB academic year consists of three distinct examination phases:

  • May/June 2027: IFB AQ-L s. This is the mid-year benchmark assessment covering the first 50% of the curriculum.
  • September/October 2027: IFB / AQ and AB Levels. Note: This is the only period where question papers are generated uniquely and superficially per student, strictly targeting their specific weak concepts identified in the mid-year exams. Students who fail this phase are barred from writing the final examinations.
  • November/December 2027: IFB / A. The final, comprehensive exit examination.

2. Pre-Examination Setup & International Protocols

  • Device & Connection: Ensure you have a stable internet connection. The portal relies on real-time uploads and AI processing.
  • Login Credentials: You must log in using your 13-digit Student ID and your 6-digit Security Code.
  • Global Synchronisation: Because IFB operates internationally, papers are heavily encrypted and unlock simultaneously at the global start time (UTC). Students in different time zones will write at the same absolute moment to prevent question paper sharing.
  • Watermarking: Every generated PDF question paper contains a unique diagonal watermark of your 13-digit Student ID. Leaking or sharing the paper online will result in immediate expulsion.

3. The Examination Process

  • Timekeeping: You must log in exactly at the scheduled global start time. Late entries will not be granted extra time.
  • Preparation: Download and print the Question Paper and the Answer Book. You may write directly onto the printed Answer Book.
  • Writing your Answers: Write clearly and legibly in black or blue ink. You must show all steps and working out.
  • Digital Answer Book: The portal will automatically populate your Answer Book with your Student ID and Subject details.

4. The Scanning & Uploading Protocol

CRITICAL: You must upload your answers within ONE (1) HOUR of the examination session ending. The portal will lock after this window closes.
  • You must scan/photograph your Answer Book pages using the portal's camera interface.
  • You are required to take at least three (3) pictures of each page to ensure clarity and prevent occlusions (shadows, fingers, glare).
  • You will be prompted to verify if the question numbers are legible. The AI will map your written answer to the specific question number you wrote.

5. AI Moderation & Marking Rules

All scripts are processed by our AI Examination Board (DeepSeek, Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude). The following rules apply strictly:

  • No "Answers Only": The AI evaluates your procedural logic. Answers without steps will be awarded ZERO marks.
  • Pattern Recognition: The AI is trained to detect if your handwriting and logic appear too "unplanned" or unnatural. If the system suspects the answer was AI-generated or memorised without understanding, the script will be flagged.
  • Human Moderation: Any flagged script will be immediately locked and an automated email will be dispatched to the Chief Examiner (n1nathaniel971@gmail.com) for manual review.
