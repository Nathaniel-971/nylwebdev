Our Mission

To provide a rigorous, accessible, and non-accredited STEM and software engineering education. We empower driven individuals to master web development and integrated systems through practical, real-world curriculum and assessment—completely free of tuition fees.

Full Stack Developer Curriculum

Mathematics is compulsory with Physics, and highly recommended alongside any other stream. These stages form our core subjects.

Stage 1 & 2

Web Foundations HTML5, CSS3, Git & GitHub

Stage 3 & 4

Programming & UI JavaScript, ES6, Bootstrap, Tailwind

Stage 5 & 6

Front-End Systems React.js, Vue.js, State Management

Stage 7 & 8

Back-End Systems Node.js, Express, APIs

Stage 9 & 10

Databases MongoDB, MySQL, Indexing

Stage 11

Deployment & Cloud AWS, Render, Vercel, CI/CD
