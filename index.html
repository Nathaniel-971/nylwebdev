Terms of Service

Last updated: 2027 Intake
Non-Accreditation Notice IFB Institute is a non-accredited, non-registered institution. Our certificates are internal records of completion and do not carry NQF credits. They are not substitutes for official qualifications.

1. Admissions

Submission of an application does not guarantee admission. All candidates must sit and pass the IFB/AE Admission Examination (January / February 2027) to be issued a Student Number and proceed to the academic year.

2. Examinations and AI

All examination papers are set by the IFB Examination Board. Marking and quality assurance are conducted by DeepSeek AI. The administration cannot manually override, edit, or award marks. Results issued by the AI are final.

3. One Sitting Rule

The IFB/AE is a single-opportunity test. Candidates who fail or are flagged for academic dishonesty (including cheating or using external AI) will have their application revoked and will not be permitted to retake the examination for the 2027 intake.

4. Curriculum and Subject Changes

Candidates may edit their curriculum (stream and electives) until 21 March 2027. After this date, the curriculum is locked for the academic year.

5. Downloads

All downloadable documents (including Admission Slips and Question Papers) are for personal use only. Redistribution is prohibited.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2027 Intake

1. Information We Collect

Upon application, we collect your Name, Email Address, ID/Passport Number, Date of Birth, Contact Number, Physical Address, and Prior Education details. Once you sit examinations, we collect your examination answers and results.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly to verify identity, issue Reference and Student Numbers, schedule examinations, generate Admission Slips, and maintain your academic record.

3. AI Processing

Your examination submissions are processed by DeepSeek AI for the purpose of marking and quality assurance. Your data is not used to train public AI models.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. As a non-accredited institution, we are not required to share your results with DHET, Umalusi, or SAQA, unless legally compelled by a court order.

5. Your Rights (POPIA)

You have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact the Administration. Note that examination records may be retained for audit purposes even after account deletion.
