An independent
professional credential.

The ACC is a rigorous, portfolio-backed qualification recognising practical competence in full stack development and applied mathematics. It mirrors the academic seriousness of A-Levels while remaining a free, non-accredited skills credential awarded by IFB.

06 — EXAMINATION

Strict. Independent. Yours.

All examinations are run independently by IFB. We do not offer registered examinations. We assure the quality of our programmes.

EXAM MARKS DATE WHO WRITES
FCC / IFB — Mathematics 150 May / June 2027 6-month final · 12-month 50%

Pass mark: 60%. Final exam weighs 60% of the overall result. Candidate Examination Numbers are 13 digits, issued on an official A4 document.

DISCLAIMER

Independent. Non-accredited.

The ACC is an independent, non-accredited professional certification awarded by IFB. It does not carry NQF credits and is not equivalent to a university degree or the National Senior Certificate (NSC).

The ACC is not accredited by the Department of Basic Education, the Department of Higher Education, or any NQF-recognised body. Candidates are advised to present it alongside their formal academic qualifications.

It is a skills certificate of free courses, verified under strict standards and policy.
