Global Start Protocol

Because IFB operates internationally, papers unlock simultaneously at the global start time (UTC). Students in different time zones write at the same absolute moment to prevent question-paper sharing. Check your local equivalent time carefully. Late entries are not permitted.

PREPARATION & PROTOCOL

What to Bring

Your 13-digit Student ID, 6-character Security Code, printed Question Paper, printed Answer Book, black or blue pens, and a non-programmable scientific calculator.

What NOT to Bring

No graphing calculators, no formula sheets, no phones (except as scanner), no name-writing on scripts. Only your Student ID barcode may appear on your work.

CRITICAL: You must upload your scanned pages within 1 hour of the session ending. The portal locks after this window. Failure to upload results in an invalid script.

HOW THE ADMISSION EXAMINATION WORKS
FOUR STEPS
  1. Register: Submit your details on admission.html. On submission, the system generates your 13-digit Student ID and 6-character Security Code. Screenshot both — you need them to enter the exam.
  2. Log in to Exam Gateway: On your scheduled date, visit exam-gateway.html and enter your Student ID + Security Code. The secure timer starts the moment you enter — no late entries.
  3. Write & Scan: Download the Question Paper and Answer Book. Write by hand, then photograph each page using the in-portal scanner. Uploads happen automatically.
  4. Anonymous Marking: Your name never appears on the script. Only your Student ID barcode is used. Scripts are AI-moderated, and any flagged script goes to the Chief Examiner.

THE 2027 ACADEMIC YEAR
May/June 2027

Mid-Year Diagnostic

Half-year benchmark covering roughly 50% of the curriculum. Feedback only — does not count toward your final result.

Oct/Nov 2027

AQ-L Assessment

150 marks across three papers, weighted at 15% of your year mark. Pass to unlock your certificate eligibility.

Dec 2027

Final Examination

Three papers, 100 marks each, full syllabus. This is the award sitting. Pass all three and the certificate is issued.

May/June 2028

Rewrite (if needed)

Three papers, 100 marks each, weighted standalone. For candidates who did not meet the pass requirements in December.
