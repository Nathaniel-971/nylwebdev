Code of Conduct

By accessing this examination, you agree to abide by the following rules. Failure to comply will result in immediate disqualification and potential legal action.

1. Academic Integrity

  • You must complete the examination entirely on your own.
  • You may not use unauthorized materials, notes, or AI assistants.
  • You may not communicate with any other person during the exam.

2. Identity Verification

  • You must present this Access Pass for verification.
  • Your Security Code is unique to you and must not be shared.

3. Technical Requirements

  • You must use a stable internet connection and a charged device.
  • If you are disconnected, the timer will continue running.

4. Malpractice

Any attempt to hack the portal, impersonate another student, or leak the examination paper will be reported to the relevant authorities under the Cybercrimes Act.

IFB REMINDERS ...

Reminders

Please read these reminders carefully before the day of your examination.

1. Timezones

The exam is scheduled for 21 January 2028 at 09:00 UTC. You must be online at this time. Convert the time to your local timezone.

2. Scanning Process

After you finish your answers on paper, use the IFB Scanner tool on your phone to capture each page. Ensure the scan is in Grayscale.

3. Uploading

Once the scan is complete, generate the PDF and upload it using the portal before the timer expires. Late uploads will not be accepted.

4. Troubleshooting

If you experience technical issues, use the chat feature on the exam portal to contact the invigilator immediately. Provide a screenshot of the error if possible.

5. Final Check

Check that all your answer pages are included in the final PDF. Incomplete submissions will result in lost marks.
