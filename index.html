Assessment Principles

04 / Standard

Marking is conducted by artificial intelligence. Human administrators hold no marking authority.

Written Only

Every answer is written, reasoned and shown step by step. Practical papers are submitted as working code.

AI Marking

DeepSeek AI serves as the primary regulatory and marking engine across all examinations.

Moderation

Claude, ChatGPT and Meta AI provide second-pass quality assurance and anomaly detection.

No Overrides

Faculty cannot award marks, alter grades or mark a candidate as passed. Their role is logistical.

Legal Status & Certification

The IFB Institute is an independent board. It is not accredited, not registered, and not aligned to the National Qualifications Framework. The IFB does not issue official certificates or qualifications, and makes no claim of equivalence to the Department of Higher Education and Training, Umalusi, or the South African Qualifications Authority.

While the Institute's frameworks draw on South African educational conventions, candidates receive an Internal Record of Completion detailing their programme, streams, subjects and examination results. This record is internal to the IFB and carries no external recognition.
