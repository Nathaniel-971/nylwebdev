Our Philosophy & Accreditation

We are a STEM institution focused entirely on what matters: your ability to build, solve, and engineer.

Why we are non-accredited

IFB is currently a non-accredited institution. We chose this path deliberately for our launch phase. Traditional accreditation processes in South Africa (like DHET and Umalusi) are incredibly slow and often force institutions to teach outdated syllabuses to meet bureaucratic standards.

Instead, we focus entirely on modern industry practices: AI integration, modern web stacks, and real-world deployment. We measure success by what you can build, not by a stamp on a piece of paper.

How you can help us become accredited

We are fully committed to eventually becoming a formally recognized institution. To achieve this, we need resources. We are entirely free for students, which means we rely on the goodwill of sponsors and donors.

  • Funding helps us hire accredited lecturers and cover the administrative costs required by the DHET.
  • Industry partnerships help us meet the infrastructure standards required for accreditation.
  • Your sponsorship directly helps bridge the gap between agile tech education and formal state recognition.
