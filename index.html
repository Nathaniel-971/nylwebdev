Assessment principles

04 — Standard

Marking is conducted by artificial intelligence. DeepSeek AI is the primary marking engine. Claude, ChatGPT and Meta AI provide second-pass moderation and anomaly detection.

Human administrators hold no marking authority. Faculty cannot award marks, override AI grading, or mark a candidate as passed. Their role is logistical.

Theory

Written, step-by-step reasoning on each programme's core topics. Method is credited, not only the final answer.

Practical

Working submissions — code, design, analysis or experiment — uploaded into the secure IDE.

No overrides

Grades are AI-issued. Human intervention is logistical only.

Legal status

05 — Notice

The IFB Institute is an independent board. It is not accredited, not registered, and not aligned to the National Qualifications Framework. The IFB Institute does not issue official certificates or qualifications, and makes no claim of equivalence to the Department of Higher Education and Training, Umalusi, or the South African Qualifications Authority.

While the Institute's frameworks draw on South African educational conventions, candidates receive an Internal Record of Completion detailing their programme, subjects and examination results. This record is internal to the IFB Institute and carries no external recognition.
