Why non-accredited?

Traditional accreditation is slow, expensive, and often years behind modern industry standards. By choosing to operate independently, we bypass the bureaucratic overhead and keep our courses 100% free.

You still get a university-level curriculum, rigorous assessments, and real-world skills. You just don't pay thousands in tuition for a government stamp that employers rarely look at anyway when you have a solid portfolio.

Zero Cost

No tuition, no exam fees. Funded entirely by sponsors and donors.

Anonymous Marking

Scripts are marked using only your Student ID barcode. Zero bias in grading.

Practical Skills

We focus on modern, industry-standard tools rather than outdated textbooks.
