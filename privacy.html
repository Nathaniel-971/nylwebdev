//Privacy & Cookies

How we handle your data. Plain language, no lawyer speak.

Cookies

This website uses a single essential cookie stored in your browser's localStorage. It records whether you have accepted or declined the cookie banner. We do not use tracking, advertising, or third-party cookies.

You can clear this at any time by clearing your browser's storage for this site.

What we collect

  • Nothing personal from your visit
  • No IP address stored
  • No cookies from Google, Meta, or advertisers
  • No form submissions stored on our server

The contact form and chat

Our contact form and chat do not send data to a server. When you click "Send via WhatsApp", your browser opens WhatsApp with your message pre-filled. You send it yourself. We only receive what you choose to send.

The Create tool

When you use the Create tool, your prompt is sent to our server which forwards it to a third-party AI provider (DeepSeek, Google Gemini, or OpenRouter). We do not store your prompt or the generated design on our server. The AI provider may log requests per their own privacy policies.

Third parties

We use Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) and Lucide Icons (unpkg.com) to display fonts and icons. These services may log your IP address when loading files — that is out of our control and governed by their own privacy policies.

Your rights

Under South Africa's POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act), you have the right to know what data is held about you, and to have it deleted. Since we don't collect or store personal data on this website, there is nothing to delete.
