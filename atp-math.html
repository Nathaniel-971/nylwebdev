CURRICULUM OVERVIEW

The IFB AE-S Mathematics curriculum is not a standard high school syllabus. It is a rigorous, university-level bridging course designed to force students to master abstract, logical, and computational thinking without relying on rote memorization or formula sheets.


Students are expected to complete approximately 10 to 12 hours of self-study per week. The curriculum is strictly divided into advanced pillars, running continuously across a 40-week academic year:

  • Advanced Algebra: Matrices, Complex Numbers, Quartic Functions, Partial Fractions, and rigid inequalities.
  • Functions & Geometry: Deep analysis of composite/inverse functions, 3D analytical geometry, and nature of roots.
  • Calculus (~140 Marks): Limits, advanced differentiation, differential equations, integration, and real-world optimization.
  • Probability & Data: Bayes' Theorem, 3-set Venn diagrams, and advanced statistics.

Assessment Structure: The course features four rigorous examination papers (Paper 1, 2, 3, and 4) totaling 600 Marks. No formula sheets are provided; students must derive or memorize all necessary mathematical identities.

FORMULA & THEOREM MANIFEST

Students are required to know and apply the following advanced formulas. No formula sheet will be provided during examinations.

Quartic & Advanced Roots
  • For \(ax^4 + bx^3 + cx^2 + dx + e = 0\), use the derivative \(f'(x) = 4ax^3 + 3bx^2 + 2cx + d\) to find turning points.
  • Nature of roots is determined by the number of times the graph crosses the \(x\)-axis: 0 (no real roots), 2 (two real, two non-real), 4 (four real roots or two double roots).
  • Double root condition: \(f(x) = 0\) and \(f'(x) = 0\) at the same \(x\)-value.
Advanced Limits & Calculus
  • L'Hôpital's Rule: If \(\lim \frac{f(x)}{g(x)} = \frac{0}{0}\), then \(\lim \frac{f(x)}{g(x)} = \lim \frac{f'(x)}{g'(x)}\).
  • Product Rule: \(\frac{d}{dx}[uv] = u'v + uv'\)
  • Quotient Rule: \(\frac{d}{dx}\left[\frac{u}{v}\right] = \frac{u'v - uv'}{v^2}\)
  • Chain Rule: \(\frac{d}{dx}[f(g(x))] = f'(g(x)) \cdot g'(x)\)
  • Integration by Parts: \(\int u \, dv = uv - \int v \, du\)
Differential Equations
  • Separable equations: \(\frac{dy}{dx} = f(x)g(y) \implies \int \frac{1}{g(y)} dy = \int f(x) dx\)
  • Exponential growth/decay: \(\frac{dy}{dt} = ky \implies y(t) = y_0 e^{kt}\)
Matrices & Complex Numbers
  • Inverse of 2x2 matrix \(A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}\): \(A^{-1} = \frac{1}{ad-bc} \begin{pmatrix} d & -b \\ -c & a \end{pmatrix}\)
  • De Moivre's Theorem: \([r(\cos \theta + i \sin \theta)]^n = r^n(\cos n\theta + i \sin n\theta)\)
