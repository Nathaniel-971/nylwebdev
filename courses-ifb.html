Duration

A brutal, university-level 4-paper curriculum that assumes Matric Math is already mastered — then pushes far beyond it. No formula sheets. No shortcuts. Pure derivation from first principles.

Curriculum Pillars

  • Algebra — quartic polynomials, complex numbers, matrices, Cramer's Rule
  • Functions — composite, inverse, transformations, logarithms
  • Sequences & Series — arithmetic, geometric, sum to infinity, sigma notation
  • Differential Calculus — first principles, product/quotient/chain rules, implicit differentiation, optimisation
  • Integral Calculus — substitution, parts, partial fractions, volumes of revolution
  • Trigonometry — compound/double angle identities, 2D/3D sine & cosine rules
  • Euclidean Geometry — circle theorems, tangents, proportionality
  • Analytical Geometry — lines, circles, vectors, dot & cross product
  • Finance — annuities, sinking funds, effective interest rates
  • Probability & Statistics — Bayes' Theorem, regression, correlation
Entry Requirement: You must pass the IFB-S Mathematics Admission Examination (AE) on 13 January 2027. Assumes prior mastery of Matric-level mathematics.

THE ADMISSION EXAMINATION (AE)

IFB does not accept everyone. Every applicant — regardless of track — must sit and pass the Admission Examination. There is no exception, no interview, no portfolio bypass. The AE is the gate.

AE Dates 2027

ExamDateDurationMarks
IFB-S Mathematics (AE-S)13 January 20272 h100
IFB AQ-B Web Dev (AE-W)18 January 20271 h50

Credentials Generated at Application

  • 13-Digit Student ID — derived from date of birth. Your permanent IFB identity.
  • 6-Digit Security Code — required to enter the Exam Gateway.
  • Anonymous Barcode — your scripts carry only this barcode, never your name.

Financial Model

IFB charges zero tuition, zero application fees, zero exam fees, and zero graduation fees. The institution is funded entirely by sponsors and donors. If you are admitted, your education is free.

How to Apply

  • Visit admission.html on the IFB portal.
  • Submit your details and select your course(s).
  • Receive your Student ID and Security Code instantly.
  • Sit the AE on your scheduled date.
  • Pass the AE and you are enrolled.
Applications close: 13 January 2027. Spaces are limited and admission is strictly conditional on passing the AE.
