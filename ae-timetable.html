How the Admission Examination Works

1

Register on the Application Portal

Submit your details via admission.html. On submission, the system instantly generates your 13-digit Student ID and 6-digit Security Code. Screenshot these — you will need them to enter the exam.

2

Log in to the Exam Gateway

On your scheduled date, visit exam-gateway.html and enter your Student ID + Security Code. The secure timer starts the moment you enter — no late entries.

3

Write & Scan Your Answers

Download and print the Question Paper and the Answer Book. Write your answers by hand, then photograph each page using the in-portal scanner. Your answers upload automatically.

4

Anonymous Marking

Your name never appears on the script. Only your Student ID barcode is used. Scripts are AI-moderated and any flagged script is sent to the Chief Examiner for human review.

No Formula Sheets

IFB-S Mathematics provides no formula sheet. All identities, theorems and formulas must be memorised or derived from first principles.

Global Start Time

Because IFB operates internationally, papers unlock simultaneously at the global start time (UTC). Check your local time zone carefully.
