The 6-month and 12-month tracks cover different content and have different exam cycles.

Jan – Jun 2027 2 exam periods

12 MONTHS

ACC

Applied Competence Certificate
Jan – Nov 2027 3 exam periods
  • First Certification Period — May/June 2027
  • Targeted Revision Period — September 2027

Each exam period contains one paper per subject. All papers are set by our department. All courses are non-accredited. The Targeted Revision Period covers only the topics you scored poorly in — it is targeted revision, not a blank re-test.
