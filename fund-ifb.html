Our Mission: The Institute of Board (IFB) is an independent, non-accredited academic academy. We provide rigorous training in Mathematics and Web Development completely fee-free. We believe that financial barriers should never stop a student from mastering high-level skills.

Where Your Sponsorship Goes

While our tuition is free, running a digital institution still requires resources. Your sponsorship directly supports:

  • Hosting & Infrastructure: Keeping our website, exam portals, and databases online.
  • Data & Connectivity: Allowing us to provide online resources and tutoring to students who lack stable internet.
  • Educational Materials: Funding the development of high-quality, AI-generated practice papers and learning resources.
  • Student Support: Helping students cover basic administrative costs related to exams or project deployments.
