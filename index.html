The IFB Philosophy

We are not just a web development bootcamp. We are a fully-fledged STEM educational institution.

Why we are non-accredited

IFB is a non-accredited institution. We chose this path deliberately. Traditional accreditation often forces institutions to teach outdated syllabuses to meet bureaucratic standards. We are not registered with DHET or Umalusi.

Instead, we focus entirely on what actually matters: modern industry practices, live code, and real-world application. We measure success by what you can build, not by a stamp on a piece of paper.

The Advantages

  • Zero tuition fees. Education should not be gatekept by wealth.
  • Curriculum updated continuously to match current tech ecosystems.
  • Deep integration of AI agents and modern deployment pipelines.
  • Pure focus on technical competence, not academic bureaucracy.
