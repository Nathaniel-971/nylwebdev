Terms of Admission
Code of Conduct & Academic Policies

By accepting this offer of admission, you agree to the following terms and conditions:

1. Academic Integrity

You must maintain the highest standards of academic honesty. Any form of cheating, plagiarism, or misrepresentation in examinations or assignments will result in immediate disciplinary action.

2. Code of Conduct

  • Respect all staff, invigilators, and fellow students.
  • Use the IFB portal only for legitimate academic purposes.
  • Do not share your Student ID or Security Code with anyone.

3. Examination Rules

All examinations are conducted online. You must use the IFB Scanner tool to upload your answers. Any attempt to impersonate another student or tamper with the portal will be reported to the authorities under the Cybercrimes Act.

4. POPIA & GDPR

Your personal data is processed in accordance with POPIA and GDPR. You may request deletion of your data at any time via the Student Portal.

5. Non-Accreditation

The IFB is a skills academy. This letter does not constitute an accredited university offer or NQF credits.
