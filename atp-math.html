COMPREHENSIVE TOPIC BREAKDOWN

The IFB-S Mathematics curriculum is an exhaustive, 40-week university-level bridge. It covers every foundational pillar of pure and applied mathematics. To answer your question: Yes, these 9 topics cover the entire spectrum. However, to truly double the content, we must dive deep into the specific sub-topics within each pillar. This requires a minimum of 1 to 2 hours of intense, independent study per day. No formula sheets are provided during any assessment.

The 9 Mathematical Pillars
Core Pillar Detailed Scope
1. Algebra Quadratics, Cubics, Quartics, Inequalities, Simultaneous Equations, Matrices, Complex Numbers, Partial Fractions.
2. Functions Notation, Domains, Inverses, Composites, and the 6 Parent Functions with full transformations.
3. Calculus Limits, Differential Rules, Curve Sketching, Optimization, Integration, Areas/Volumes, Differential Equations.
4. Trigonometry Identities, Compound/Double Angles, General Solutions, Graphs, 2D/3D Sine/Cosine/Area Rules, Bearings.
5. Euclidean Geometry Circle Theorems, Tangents, Cyclic Quadrilaterals, Similar Triangles, Proportionality, Midpoint Theorem.
6. Analytical Geometry Distance/Gradient/Midpoint, Lines, Circles, Tangents, Intersections, 2D/3D Vectors, Dot/Cross Products.
7. Finance Simple/Compound Interest, Depreciation, Nominal/Effective Rates, Annuities, Sinking Funds, Bonds.
8. Probability 2/3-Set Venn Diagrams, Tree Diagrams, Independent/Dependent Events, Mutually Exclusive, Bayes' Theorem.
9. Statistics Ogives, Box Plots, Quartiles, IQR, Standard Deviation, Variance, Regression Analysis, Correlation.

ELABORATION OF CONTENT (WEEKS 1-12)
Week Topic Elaboration of Content Assess
1 Exponents & Surds
  • Laws of exponents (rational, negative, and zero exponents).
  • Simplifying and rationalising surds (monomial and binomial denominators).
  • Solving exponential equations (same base and quadratic substitution).
2 Quadratic Equations
  • Factorisation, completing the square, and the quadratic formula.
  • Nature of roots using the discriminant (\(\Delta = b^2 - 4ac\)): equal, real, non-real.
  • Finding values of \(k\) for specific root conditions.
3 Cubic & Quartic Polynomials
  • Remainder and Factor Theorem for degrees 3 and 4.
  • Factorising \(ax^3 + bx^2 + cx + d\) and \(ax^4 + \dots\)
  • Using synthetic division to find roots.
4 Simultaneous Equations
  • Solving 1 linear and 1 quadratic equation algebraically and graphically.
  • Solving 3 linear equations with 3 unknowns using substitution/elimination.
  • Word problems resulting in simultaneous equations.
5 Advanced Inequalities
  • Quadratic inequalities (parabolic method).
  • Rational inequalities using critical values and sign tables.
  • Absolute value equations and inequalities.
6 Matrices & Complex Numbers
  • Matrix operations (addition, multiplication, determinants, inverses).
  • Solving 2x2 and 3x3 systems using Cramer's Rule.
  • Complex numbers: Argand diagrams, \(i^2 = -1\), and basic arithmetic.
7 Function Notation & Domain
  • Understanding \(f(x)\), \(f: x \mapsto f(x)\), and interval notation.
  • Finding domain and range algebraically and graphically.
  • Identifying restrictions on rational and radical functions.
8 Composite & Inverse Functions
  • Evaluating \(f(g(x))\) and \(g(f(x))\). Domain/range of composites.
  • One-to-one functions and finding the inverse \(f^{-1}(x)\).
  • Graphing inverses and restricting the domain for parabolas.
9 The 6 Parent Functions
  • Linear, Quadratic, Cubic, Hyperbolic, Exponential, Logarithmic.
  • Base graphs without transformations to understand asymptotes and shape.
10 Transformations & Shifts
  • Applying the general form \(y = a \cdot f(x-p) + q\).
  • Vertical and horizontal shifts, reflections, and stretches.
  • Finding intercepts and asymptotes of shifted functions.
11 Exponential & Log Graphs
  • Deep dive into the inverse relationship of \(y=a^x\) and \(y=\log_a x\).
  • Log laws and solving exponential/log equations with shifts.
12 Task 1 Assessment
  • Synthesis of algebra, composites, inverses, and 6 shifted functions.

Week Topic Elaboration of Content Assess
13 Limits & First Principles
  • Informal and formal concept of a limit. One-sided limits.
  • First principles: \(f'(x) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}\).
  • Applying first principles to linear, quadratic, and cubic functions.
14 Differentiation Rules (1)
  • Power rule for polynomials, fractional exponents, and surds.
  • Constant rule and sum/difference rules.
  • Finding the derivative of simple trigonometric functions.
15 Differentiation Rules (2)
  • Product rule: \(\frac{d}{dx}[uv] = u'v + uv'\).
  • Quotient rule: \(\frac{d}{dx}\left[\frac{u}{v}\right] = \frac{u'v - uv'}{v^2}\).
  • Chain rule: \(\frac{d}{dx}[f(g(x))] = f'(g(x)) \cdot g'(x)\).
16 Implicit & Higher-Order
  • Implicit differentiation for equations not in \(y=...\) form.
  • Finding the second derivative \(f''(x)\).
  • Using the second derivative to determine concavity.
17 Applications (1)
  • Equations of tangents and normals.
  • Finding stationary points (where \(f'(x)=0\)).
  • Classifying stationary points using \(f''(x)\) (max, min, inflection).
18 Applications (2)
  • Curve sketching: combining intercepts, asymptotes, and turning points.
  • Sketching cubic and quartic functions.
19 Optimization & Rates
  • Real-world optimization problems (maximizing volume, minimizing area/cost).
  • Solving related rates problems (e.g., expanding balloons, sliding ladders).
20 Task 2 Assessment
  • Synthesis of differentiation rules and applications.

ELABORATION OF CONTENT (WEEKS 21-26)
Week Topic Elaboration of Content Assess
21 Anti-Differentiation
  • The concept of an indefinite integral.
  • Power rule for integration: \(\int x^n dx = \frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1} + C\).
  • Integrating polynomials, fractional powers, and basic trig/exponential functions.
22 Definite Integrals
  • The Fundamental Theorem of Calculus.
  • Evaluating definite integrals \(\int_{a}^{b} f(x) dx\).
  • Properties of definite integrals.
23 Integration Techniques
  • Integration by substitution (u-substitution).
  • Integration by parts: \(\int u \, dv = uv - \int v \, du\).
  • Integrating partial fractions.
24 Applications: Area
  • Calculating the area under a curve between two \(x\)-values.
  • Calculating the area between two curves \(f(x)\) and \(g(x)\).
  • Area bounded by the \(y\)-axis.
25 Applications: Volume
  • Volumes of solids of revolution around the x-axis: \(V = \pi \int_{a}^{b} [f(x)]^2 dx\).
  • Volumes of solids of revolution around the y-axis.
26 Differential Equations
  • Solving first-order separable differential equations: \(\frac{dy}{dx} = f(x)g(y)\).
  • Exponential growth and decay models: \(\frac{dy}{dt} = ky\).

Week Topic Elaboration of Content Assess
27 Trig Identities & Equations
  • Compound angle, double angle, and half-angle identities.
  • Proving complex trigonometric identities.
  • Solving general solutions for trigonometric equations.
28 Trig Graphs & Applications
  • Sketching trigonometric graphs with transformations (amplitude, period, shifts).
  • Sine rule, cosine rule, and area rule in 2D and 3D.
  • Bearings and angles of elevation/depression.
29 Circle Theorems
  • Angle at centre = 2x angle at circumference.
  • Angle in semi-circle = 90 degrees.
  • Cyclic quadrilaterals (opposite angles supplementary).
30 Tangents & Similar Triangles
  • Tangent perpendicular to radius.
  • Tan-chord theorem and alternate segment theorem.
  • Proving triangles are similar (equiangular, proportional sides).
31 Proportionality & Midpoint
  • Line parallel to one side of a triangle divides the other two proportionally.
  • Midpoint theorem and its converse.
  • Pythagorean theorem proofs using similar triangles.
32 Lines & Circles
  • Distance, gradient, midpoint formulas.
  • Equation of a line, parallel/perpendicular lines, angle of inclination.
  • Equation of a circle \((x-a)^2 + (y-b)^2 = r^2\) and tangents to circles.
33 Vectors (2D & 3D)
  • Vector notation, addition, subtraction, scalar multiplication.
  • Dot product (scalar product) and angles between vectors.
  • Cross product (vector product) and its applications in 3D space.
