INSTITUTIONAL OVERVIEW
PLEASE READ CAREFULLY
Non-Accredited Institution: The Institute of Board is an independent, non-accredited academic institution. We are not registered with the DHET or Umalusi. Our programmes focus purely on rigorous technical and academic mastery, portfolio building, and university preparation.

The Institute of Board (IFB) provides an alternative pathway for students who want to build deep, practical skills outside of the traditional, expensive university system.

We operate on a fee-free basis, allowing us to focus purely on modern, advanced academic skills. Our curriculum is designed to bridge the gap between high school and higher education or the tech industry.

Admissions Pathway

01
Apply & Register: Sign in with Google and complete the application to register as an IFB student.
02
Sit the AE Paper: Write the AE Paper (100 marks, 2 hours) covering all high school mathematics.
03
Get your Exam Number: Pass the AE to be issued your permanent 13-digit Examination Number and unlock your dashboard.
04
Begin studying: Access the rigorous IFB curriculum, study resources, and prepare for final exit exams.
