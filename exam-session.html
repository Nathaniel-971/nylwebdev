How to scan your answer pages

  • Select a question, then open the camera.
  • Fill the frame with one whole A4 page.
  • Hold steady, straight above the paper.
  • The flash fires automatically — no dark shots.
  • The page is straightened to A4 and text is enhanced.
  • Add more pages for long answers — they stack.
