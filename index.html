Admissions Pathway

How it works

Applying registers you as a student. You must then sit the AE Paper.

1

Apply & Register

Sign in with Google and complete the application. This formally registers you as an IFB student.

2

Sit the AE Paper

Write the AE Paper (100 marks, 2 hours) covering all high school mathematics. This is your entrance exam.

3

Get your Exam Number

Pass the AE to be issued your permanent 13-digit Examination Number and unlock your dashboard.

4

Begin studying

Access the rigorous 1-year IFB— Mathematics curriculum, study resources, and prepare for final exit exams.
