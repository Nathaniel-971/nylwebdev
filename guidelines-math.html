1. Paper Structure

The IFB-S Mathematics final examination consists of four papers totalling 600 marks. All four papers must be written.

PaperContentMarks
Paper 1Advanced Algebra, Quartics & Functions150
Paper 2Differential Calculus (75) & Integral Calculus (75)150
Paper 3Trigonometry, Geometry & Probability150
Paper 4Synthesis & Real-World Applications150
No formula sheets are provided during ANY assessment. All identities, theorems, and formulas must be derived from first principles or memorised perfectly.

2. Assessment Phases

May/June 2027 — IFB AQ-L s

Mid-year benchmark assessment covering approximately 50% of the curriculum. This is diagnostic, not final.

September/October 2027 — IFB / AQ and AB Levels

Question papers are generated uniquely and superficially per student, based on their specific weak concepts from the mid-year assessments. Students failing this phase are barred from writing the final examinations.

November/December 2027 — IFB / A (Final)

The comprehensive exit examination covering all 9 pillars. This is the only assessment that determines certification.

3. Marking Rubric

All scripts are evaluated by the IFB AI Examination Board. Key rules:

  • No "answers only": Answers without working out are awarded ZERO marks.
  • Method marks: Each question is decomposed into method, accuracy, and final-answer marks.
  • Follow-through marking: Arithmetic errors are penalised once, not repeatedly in follow-on steps.
  • Alternative methods: Any mathematically valid approach receives full credit.
  • Pattern recognition: The AI detects unnatural or AI-generated handwriting patterns. Flagged scripts go to human review.

4. Answer Book Requirements

  • Use the official IFB Answer Book (23 pages). Download from the Resources page.
  • Write only in black or blue ink. Pencil answers are not marked.
  • Write only on the right-hand pages. Left margins are for markers.
  • Do not write your name anywhere on the script — only your Student ID barcode.
  • Number your answers exactly as numbered in the question paper.

5. Calculator Policy

An approved scientific calculator (non-programmable and non-graphical) is permitted for all papers. Graphing calculators and CAS-enabled devices are strictly prohibited.

6. Scanning & Upload

After the exam session, you must scan your answer book pages using the in-portal scanner within 1 hour of the session ending. The portal locks after this window.
