How it works

From application to certification

A single, rigorous pathway. Every applicant goes through the same gate.

1

Apply online

Sign in with Google, complete the admission form, and instantly receive your 6-digit Student ID and security code.

2

Sit the AE

Write the Admission Examination online on your scheduled date. Pass the AE to be issued your 13-digit Examination Number.

3

Learn & assess

Follow the structured curriculum. Continuous and mid-year assessments track your progress across all topics.

4

Final examinations

Sit the terminal IFB/Q Examination. Achieve the pass mark and receive your QCA/ICS certification.
