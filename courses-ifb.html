IFB-S · MATHEMATICS

IFB-S Mathematics

Not standard high school math. A brutal, university-level 4-paper curriculum that assumes Matric Math already in hand — and then pushes far beyond it.

Curriculum Pillars

  • Algebra — quartic polynomials, complex numbers, matrices, Cramer's Rule
  • Functions — composite, inverse, transformations, logarithms
  • Sequences & Series — arithmetic, geometric, sum to infinity, sigma notation
  • Differential Calculus — first principles, product/quotient/chain rules, implicit differentiation, optimisation
  • Integral Calculus — substitution, parts, partial fractions, volumes of revolution
  • Trigonometry — compound/double angle identities, 2D/3D sine & cosine rules
  • Euclidean Geometry — circle theorems, tangents, proportionality
  • Analytical Geometry — lines, circles, vectors, dot & cross product
  • Finance — annuities, sinking funds, effective interest rates
  • Probability & Statistics — Bayes' Theorem, regression, correlation

Entry Requirement

You must pass the IFB-S Mathematics Admission Examination (AE) on 13 January 2027. Assumes prior mastery of Matric-level mathematics.

The Admission Examination (AE)

IFB does not accept everyone. Every applicant — regardless of track — must sit and pass the Admission Examination. There is no exception, no interview, no portfolio bypass. The AE is the gate.

13-Digit Student ID

Generated at application time, derived from your date of birth. This is your identity inside IFB — your name never appears on your answer script.

6-Digit Security Code

A second credential, required to enter the Exam Gateway. Used to authenticate you at the start of every examination session.

Anonymous Barcode Marking

Every script is stamped with your Student ID barcode only. Markers never see a name. This is designed to eliminate bias in grading.

AI Moderation

Scripts are processed by the IFB AI Examination Board. Any script flagged for irregularity is locked and escalated to the Chief Examiner for human review.

The Financial Model
