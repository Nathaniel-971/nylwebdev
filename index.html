02 / Process

A rigid, transparent pathway.

We do not accept prior grades as a proxy for readiness. Every applicant must sit the IFB/AE, a written assessment designed to test foundational logic, comprehension, and numerical reasoning.

The IFB Institute is an independent board. It is not accredited, not registered, and not aligned to the National Qualifications Framework. The IFB Institute does not issue official certificates or qualifications, and makes no claim of equivalence to the Department of Higher Education and Training, Umalusi, or the South African Qualifications Authority. Candidates receive an Internal Record of Completion detailing their programme, subjects and examination results.
