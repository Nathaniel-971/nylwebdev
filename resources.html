Document Naming Convention

Every document on this page follows a strict naming pattern so you can always find what you're looking for. Search the filename in your device's Files app and it will appear immediately.

IFB-{YEAR}-{TRACK}-{TYPE}-{SESSION}-{PAPER}.{ext}

Track

MATHMathematics

Type

PPPast Paper
MEMOMemorandum
MOCKMock Paper
GLSubject Guidelines
ATPAnnual Teaching Plan
ABAnswer Book
INFOInformation Sheet

Session (exams only)

JANJanuary (AE)
JUNJune (Interim)
SEPSeptember (AQ-L)
NOVNovember (IFB/Q)

Paper (multi-paper only)

P1Paper 1
P2Paper 2
P3Paper 3
P4Paper 4

Examples

IFB-2027-MATH-PP-JAN.pdf — Admission Exam question paper IFB-2027-MATH-MEMO-JAN.pdf — Admission Exam memo IFB-2027-MATH-PP-NOV-P1.pdf — November Q Examination, Paper 1 IFB-2027-MATH-MEMO-NOV-P1.pdf — November Paper 1 memo IFB-2027-MATH-MOCK-P1.pdf — Mock Paper 1 IFB-2027-MATH-GL-GENERAL.pdf — Subject guidelines IFB-2027-MATH-ATP.pdf — Annual Teaching Plan IFB-2027-MATH-AB-TEMPLATE.pdf — Answer Book template
