Explore the rigorous, fee-free programmes offered by the Institute of Board.

Non-Accredited Status: The Institute of Board is an independent, non-accredited academic institution. We are not registered with the DHET or Umalusi. Our programmes focus purely on rigorous technical and academic mastery, portfolio building, and university preparation.

IFB— Mathematics

Programme Code: IFB-MATH

Programme Overview

This is a high-cognitive curriculum designed for students who want to master mathematics beyond the standard high school level. It requires absolute focus and a willingness to engage with complex problem-solving. Entry is assessed entirely via the AE Paper.

Core Modules

Advanced Algebra & Functions

Equations, inequalities, polynomials, and the behavior of functions.

Calculus

Limits, derivatives, integration, and optimization.

Trigonometry & Geometry

Identities, solving triangles, Euclidean geometry, and analytical geometry.

Finance & Probability

Compound interest, annuities, and statistical probability models.

Duration 1 Year
Admission Requirement AE Paper (100 Marks)
Tuition Fee-Free

IFB— Web Development

Programme Code: IFB-WEB

Programme Overview

A practical, project-based programme focusing on back-end web development and website management. You will learn how to build, deploy, and manage real web applications, moving beyond theory into practical execution.

Core Modules

Back-End Development

Server-side logic, databases, and handling data securely.

API Handling

Connecting applications to third-party services and managing data flow.

Design & Deployment

UI design principles, deploying to production servers, and website management.

Data Collection

Understanding how to structure, store, and retrieve data effectively.

Duration 1 Year
Admission Requirement AE Paper (100 Marks)
Tuition Fee-Free
