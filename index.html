What our record does

Our certificate is an internal, non‑accredited record of completion. It confirms that a student took part in our programme and passed our internal assessments.

  • Records programme, stream, subjects and electives
  • Shows exam periods sat and internal results
  • Gives an internal student and examination number
  • Supports a CV or portfolio alongside real work
  • Verifiable through our internal student portal

What our record does not do

It is not an official qualification. It is not registered with DHET, Umalusi, SAQA or the NQF. It does not carry credits and cannot be used as a substitute for a registered qualification.

  • Not accredited · not NQF‑registered
  • Not a matric equivalent · no university admission
  • No credits, no exemptions, no licences
  • Not recognised for regulated professions
  • No guarantee of job, visa or income
