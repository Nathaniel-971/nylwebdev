Programmes

Three durations. One structure.

Every programme follows the same subject logic — Mathematics, two core subjects, one stream subject, and two electives you choose. Duration decides how much time you get between examination periods, not what you learn.

3months

Foundation

A compact introduction. One examination period to demonstrate foundational skill across a narrowed subject set.

  • 1 exam period — Foundation Exam
  • 4 subjects
  • Stream + 1 elective
  • Entry-level portfolio
9months

Specialisation

The mainstream track. Two examination periods, a targeted revision window, and a full subject load.

  • 2 exam periods — Intermediate I & II
  • 6 subjects
  • Stream + 2 electives
  • Capstone project
Full track
12months

Advanced

The deepest track. Three examination periods, including a dedicated targeted revision cycle before the final.

  • 3 exam periods — QL, Interm, QIF
  • 6 subjects
  • Stream + 2 electives
  • Extended capstone

The Prelude

One test. Eight skills.

Before you receive a Student Number, you sit the Prelude — a single sitting that measures the base you bring. It is not a bar to entry so much as a map: your result shapes the revision plan you receive inside the portal.

  1. Sign up

    Register with email and password. The institute issues a Reference ID immediately.

  2. Sit the Prelude

    An online assessment covering eight foundational skill areas. One sitting, monitored.

  3. Receive your Student Number

    On a pass, IFB assigns your permanent Student Number and unlocks the curriculum builder.

  4. Build your curriculum

    Choose your stream and two electives. Edit as many times as you like before 21 March 2027.

What the Prelude measures

Eight skill areas

  • Numerical reasoning — arithmetic, ratios, basic algebra
  • Language comprehension — reading and inference
  • Logical reasoning — deduction and pattern
  • Computational thinking — sequencing, conditionals, decomposition
  • Digital literacy — files, browsers, tools
  • Spatial reasoning — layout and structure
  • Written communication — clarity in short form
  • Problem framing — recognising the real question

Certificate status

What our record does and does not do.

Our certificate is an internal, non-accredited record of completion. It is not an official qualification.

What it does

It confirms that a student took part in our programme and passed our internal assessments.

  • Records programme, stream, subjects and electives
  • Shows exam periods sat and internal results
  • Gives an internal Student Number and examination number
  • Supports a CV or portfolio alongside real work
  • Verifiable through the internal student portal

What it does not do

It is not registered with DHET, Umalusi, SAQA or the NQF. It does not carry credits and is not a substitute for a registered qualification.

  • Not accredited · not NQF-registered
  • Not a matric equivalent · no university admission
  • No credits, no exemptions, no licences
  • Not recognised for regulated professions
  • No guarantee of job, visa, or income
