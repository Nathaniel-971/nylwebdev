Terms of Service

Last updated: 2027 Intake
Non-Accreditation NoticeIFB Institute is a non-accredited, non-registered institution. Our certificates are internal records of completion and do not carry NQF credits.

1. Admissions

Submission of an application does not guarantee admission. All candidates must sit and pass the IFB/AE Admission Examination (January / February 2027) to be issued a Student Number.

2. Examinations and AI

All examination papers are set by the IFB Examination Board. Marking and quality assurance are conducted by DeepSeek AI. The administration cannot manually override, edit, or award marks.

3. One Sitting Rule

The IFB/AE is a single-opportunity test. Candidates who fail or are flagged for academic dishonesty will have their application revoked and will not be permitted to retake the examination.

4. Curriculum

Candidates may edit their curriculum (stream and electives) until 21 March 2027. After this date, the curriculum is locked for the academic year.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2027 Intake

1. Information We Collect

Upon application, we collect your Name, Email Address, ID/Passport Number, Date of Birth, Contact Number, Physical Address, and Prior Education details. Once you sit examinations, we collect your answers and results.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly to verify identity, issue Reference and Student Numbers, schedule examinations, generate Admission Slips, and maintain your academic record.

3. AI Processing

Your examination submissions are processed by DeepSeek AI for the purpose of marking and quality assurance. Your data is not used to train public AI models.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. As a non-accredited institution, we are not required to share your results with DHET, Umalusi, or SAQA, unless legally compelled.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data. Contact the Administration to exercise these rights.
