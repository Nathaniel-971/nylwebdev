General Rules & Policies

The binding rules, mission, privacy policy, and terms governing the Institute of Board.

1. Mission & Identity

The Institute of Board ("IFB") exists to provide elite, rigorous, university-level technical education to anyone who passes the entry requirements, completely 100% fee-free.

IFB is an independent, non-accredited academic institution. We deliberately chose this path to avoid the slow, outdated bureaucracy of DHET and Umalusi. We focus purely on modern, industry-standard skills.

Non-Accredited Status: IFB is not registered with the Department of Higher Education and Training (DHET) or Umalusi. Our certificates are internal awards, not government-recognised qualifications.

2. Admission

Admission to IFB is strictly conditional on passing the Admission Examination (AE). Every applicant must sit and pass the AE before being enrolled. There is no exception, no portfolio bypass, and no interview override.

  • The Mathematics AE takes place on 13 January 2027.
  • The Web Development AE takes place on 18 January 2027.
  • Applicants who fail are not admitted and may reapply in the following academic year.

3. Tuition & Financial Model

IFB charges zero tuition, zero application fees, zero exam fees, and zero graduation fees. The institution is funded entirely by sponsors and donors. If you are admitted, your education is free.

4. Code of Conduct

All enrolled students are expected to maintain the highest standards of academic integrity. The following are strictly prohibited:

  • Sharing, distributing, or leaking question papers online or to any third party.
  • Submitting another person's work as your own (plagiarism).
  • Using unauthorised materials during an examination session.
  • Attempting to circumvent the AI moderation system.
  • Sharing your Student ID or Security Code with anyone else.

Violations result in immediate script invalidation and potential permanent expulsion from IFB.

5. Privacy Policy

IFB collects only the minimum information necessary to operate your student account:

  • Full name, email address, date of birth, and phone number for account creation and identity verification.
  • Country for timezone alignment during examinations.
  • Selected courses for curriculum management.

All data is stored securely using Google Firebase, encrypted in transit and at rest. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

To request deletion of your account and all associated data, use the "Deregister & Delete Account" option in your Student Dashboard. Data is permanently removed within 24 hours of confirmation.

6. Terms of Service

Eligibility

You must be at least 16 years old to apply. By applying, you confirm all information you submit is accurate and truthful.

Examinations

Examinations are conducted online through the IFB Examination Portal. You are responsible for having a stable internet connection, a functioning camera, and a device capable of running the portal. IFB is not liable for technical failures on the student's side.

Anonymous Marking

All answer scripts are marked anonymously using only your Student ID barcode. Your name never appears on the script. This is a deliberate design choice to eliminate bias.

AI Moderation

Scripts are processed by the IFB AI Examination Board. Any script flagged for irregularity is locked and escalated to the Chief Examiner for human review. The Chief Examiner's decision is final.

Intellectual Property

All question papers, answer books, teaching plans, and course materials are the intellectual property of IFB. Redistribution without permission is prohibited.

Termination

IFB reserves the right to terminate any account for violation of these terms, non-payment of (zero) fees, or any conduct deemed harmful to the institution or other students.
