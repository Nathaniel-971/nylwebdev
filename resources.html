    How these A4 templates work

    Type A — Printable Templates (download & print before exam): The Answer Book template and the Mock Papers are A4 documents you print at home. You write on the printed answer book during the actual exam.

    Type B — Live Exam Content (on screen only): The real AE question paper is not a downloadable file. It appears inside the Exam Session after you enter your credentials. This prevents sharing and cheating.

    The flow: Print answer book → practice with mock papers → on exam day, log into Exam Gateway → real paper appears on screen → write on your printed answer book → scan and upload each page.
