Assessment principles

05 — Standard

Marking is conducted by artificial intelligence. DeepSeek AI is the primary regulatory and marking engine across all examinations. Claude, ChatGPT and Meta AI provide second-pass moderation and anomaly detection.

Human administrators hold no marking authority. Faculty cannot award marks, override AI grading, or mark a candidate as passed. Their role is logistical: granting paper access, releasing results and managing student data.

Written only

Every answer shows method and logic. Practical papers are working code.

No multiple choice

No paper in the Institute contains a multiple-choice question.

No overrides

Grades are AI-issued. Human intervention is logistical only.

Legal status

06 — Notice

The IFB Institute is an independent board. It is not accredited, not registered, and not aligned to the National Qualifications Framework. The IFB does not issue official certificates or qualifications, and makes no claim of equivalence to the Department of Higher Education and Training, Umalusi, or the South African Qualifications Authority.

While the Institute's frameworks draw on South African educational conventions, candidates receive an Internal Record of Completion detailing their programme, streams, subjects and examination results. This record is internal to the IFB and carries no external recognition.
