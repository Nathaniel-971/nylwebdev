Terms & Conditions
For Provisional Acceptance

By accepting this provisional offer, you acknowledge and agree to the following:

1. Provisional Status

This letter confirms provisional acceptance only. Final admission is strictly conditional on the student writing the Admissions Examination (AE) on 21 January 2027 and achieving the required pass mark.

2. Course Reference

Each programme has a unique 5-digit reference number. You must quote this number in all correspondence with the Admissions Department.

3. Academic Integrity

You must maintain the highest standards of academic honesty. Any form of cheating, plagiarism, or impersonation in the AE will result in immediate disqualification.

Your personal data is processed in accordance with POPIA and GDPR. You may request deletion of your data at any time via the Student Portal.

Code of Conduct
Student Responsibilities

1. Academic Honesty

Students must complete all examinations and assignments independently. Any attempt to use unauthorized materials or AI tools during the AE will be treated as malpractice.

2. Examination Rules

All examinations are conducted online via the IFB Portal. Students must use the IFB Scanner tool to submit their handwritten answers. Late submissions are not accepted.

3. Respect & Communication

Students must communicate respectfully with staff, invigilators, and fellow students. Abusive language or harassment will lead to immediate disciplinary action.

4. Account Security

Your Student ID, Exam Number, and Security Code are strictly confidential. Sharing these credentials with another person will result in disqualification.
