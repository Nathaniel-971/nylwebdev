Sponsor the Institute

IFB is a 100% fee-free institution. We rely entirely on the generosity of sponsors and donors to cover our hosting, software, and examination administration costs.

Why we are free

We believe that world-class technical education should not be gatekept by wealth. Every student who passes our rigorous Admission Examination is granted free access to our curriculum and resources.

Where your money goes

Sponsorships directly fund the IFB platform, including our hosting, domain registration, and the maintenance of our examination portals.

Make a Donation

We do not process payments through this website. If you would like to sponsor a student or make a contribution, please contact our administration directly to arrange a direct bank transfer.
