CURRICULUM MANIFESTO

The IFB AE-S Mathematics curriculum is an uncompromising, university-level bridging course. It operates at 100% capacity. It is designed to eradicate rote learning, forcing students to master abstract logic, rigorous proof, and advanced computational thinking.


Workload: Students must commit to a minimum of 15 hours of independent study per week. There are no formula sheets. Everything must be derived or memorized.


Assessment: Four 2-hour papers totaling 600 Marks.

  • Paper 1: Pure Algebra, Matrices & Advanced Functions.
  • Paper 2: Differential Calculus (35±5) & Integral Calculus (35±5).
  • Paper 3: Trigonometry, 3D Geometry & Probability.
  • Paper 4: Synthesis & Real-World Applications.

MODULE 1: PURE ALGEBRA & FUNCTIONS
Week Topic Content & Sub-topics
1 - 2 Polynomials & Partial Fractions Factor & Remainder Theorem for higher degree polynomials. Decomposition of rational expressions into partial fractions (linear, repeated, and irreducible quadratic denominators).
3 - 4 Quartic & Quintic Roots Analyzing \(f(x) = ax^4 + bx^3 + cx^2 + dx + e\). Sketching. Determining nature of roots (0, 2, 4 real roots, or double roots). Introduction to the Fundamental Theorem of Algebra.
5 - 6 Advanced Inequalities Rational and absolute value inequalities. Sign tables and critical values. Introduction to Linear Programming and feasible regions.
7 - 8 Matrices & Systems Solving 3x3 (and larger) systems using Gaussian Elimination and Cramer's Rule. Inverse matrices. Determinants. Matrix transformations.
9 - 10 Complex Numbers Argand diagrams. Polar form. Euler's formula \(e^{i\theta} = \cos\theta + i\sin\theta\). De Moivre's Theorem for roots of unity.

FORMULA & THEOREM MANIFEST

Students are required to know and apply the following advanced formulas. No formula sheet will be provided during examinations.

Calculus
  • First Principles: \(f'(x) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}\)
  • Product Rule: \(\frac{d}{dx}[uv] = u'v + uv'\)
  • Quotient Rule: \(\frac{d}{dx}\left[\frac{u}{v}\right] = \frac{u'v - uv'}{v^2}\)
  • Chain Rule: \(\frac{d}{dx}[f(g(x))] = f'(g(x)) \cdot g'(x)\)
  • Integration by Parts: \(\int u \, dv = uv - \int v \, du\)
  • L'Hôpital's Rule: If \(\lim \frac{f(x)}{g(x)} = \frac{0}{0}\), then \(\lim \frac{f'(x)}{g'(x)}\)
  • Volumes of Revolution: \(V = \pi \int_{a}^{b} [f(x)]^2 dx\)
Algebra & Matrices
  • Quadratic: \(x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}\)
  • Inverse 2x2 Matrix: \(A^{-1} = \frac{1}{ad-bc} \begin{pmatrix} d & -b \\ -c & a \end{pmatrix}\)
  • Euler's Formula: \(e^{i\theta} = \cos\theta + i\sin\theta\)
  • De Moivre: \([r(\cos \theta + i \sin \theta)]^n = r^n(\cos n\theta + i \sin n\theta)\)
Trigonometry & Geometry
  • Sine Rule: \(\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C}\)
  • Cosine Rule: \(a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc \cos A\)
  • Area Rule: Area \(= \frac{1}{2}ab \sin C\)
  • Cross Product: \(\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} = |\mathbf{a}||\mathbf{b}|\sin\theta \mathbf{n}\)
  • Dot Product: \(\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = |\mathbf{a}||\mathbf{b}|\cos\theta\)
