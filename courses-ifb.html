ACADEMIC PROSPECTUS
Institute of Board (IFB)
Non-Accredited Institution: The Institute of Board is an independent, non-accredited academic institution. We are not registered with the DHET or Umalusi. Our programmes focus purely on rigorous technical and academic mastery, portfolio building, and university preparation.

IFB— Mathematics

IFB-MATH
This is a high-cognitive curriculum designed for students who want to master mathematics beyond the standard high school level. It requires absolute focus and a willingness to engage with complex problem-solving. Entry is assessed entirely via the AE Paper.
Advanced Algebra & Functions
Calculus (Derivatives & Integrals)
Trigonometry & Geometry
Finance & Probability
Duration1 Year
AdmissionAE Paper (100 Marks)
TuitionFee-Free

IFB— Web Development

IFB-WEB
A practical, project-based programme focusing on back-end web development and website management. You will learn how to build, deploy, and manage real web applications, moving beyond theory into practical execution.
Back-End Development
API Handling & Data
Design & Deployment
Website Management
Duration1 Year
AdmissionAE Paper (100 Marks)
TuitionFee-Free
